Guides

Plot a trip map from the list you already have

These notes are for travelers who already know the stops — in a spreadsheet, a Google Sheet, a cruise port list, a city list, or a rail stop list — and want that itinerary on a map in the order they planned. They are not for reordering addresses into a delivery route.

  • Plot trip stops on a map from a spreadsheet (CSV or Excel)

    Drop a CSV or Excel of your trip stops — date and place columns — and see the itinerary on a map. Built for travelers, not delivery routes.

  • Trip Stops CSV Template (Date, City, State, Country)

    Download a simple trip-stops CSV — date and place columns — ready to drop into MemoryMap.

  • Map a trip from a Google Sheet (paste a link or download Excel)

    Turn a Google Sheet of trip stops into a map. Paste a viewable link or download Excel and drop it. Built for travelers keeping their planned order.

  • Turn a cruise port list into a trip map

    Plot embarkation, ports, and return from a simple port list or spreadsheet onto a map — keep sailing order, not a delivery route.

  • Road trip map from a list of cities (keep your order)

    Turn your city list into a road trip map in the order you planned — visualization, not a delivery optimizer.

  • Map a multi-city rail trip from a stop list

    Plot your rail-pass cities and dates on a map — list in, itinerary out. Not a ticket planner.

  • Turn visited places into a souvenir trip map

    Plot the places you visited — or will visit — from a simple list and download a souvenir map file. Free on MemoryMap’s public preview.

  • Shareable trip map for Instagram Stories (from your stop list)

    Plot your real stop list, then screenshot or capture a frame to post on Stories or feed. MemoryMap does not auto-post to Instagram.

  • Post your upcoming trip itinerary as a map

    Announce the trip with a real map of upcoming stops — plot the list, then share the image yourself. No auto-post required.

  • Tour operator itinerary map from a stop list

    Plot the ports and cities you already sell into a trip map for social and decks. Fast list→map on the free preview — not a tourism CMS.

  • MemoryMap vs Google My Maps for a trip list

    Compare Google My Maps and MemoryMap for spreadsheet trip itineraries — import-heavy custom maps vs list→map.

Ready to try it? Open MemoryMap and drop a spreadsheet on the homepage, paste a viewable Google Sheets link, or use a sample trip.
