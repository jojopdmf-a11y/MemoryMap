Announcing a trip lands better as geography

Tickets booked. The group chat or Instagram wants the shape of the trip. Photo dumps hide the upcoming route; animated recaps are usually after return. Pre-trip: take the planning list and turn it into something worth posting once.

Build the announcement map from the list you already have

One stop per row with a date and a place. Keep order by date. City- or port-level is enough, and usually wiser for privacy. Use the CSV template or paste a viewable Google Sheets link. Spreadsheet drop is the same path as plot trip stops from a spreadsheet.

Plot on MemoryMap, then share your way

  1. Open https://www.memorymap.world.
  2. Drop a file, paste a viewable Sheets link, or type the stops.
  3. Plot and review the upcoming itinerary on the map.
  4. Share yourself: screenshot the plotted map, or open the souvenir HTML file (when offered) and capture a clean frame. There is no “MemoryMap posts for you” button.

For Story framing and screenshot ratios, see the sibling Instagram Stories trip map guide.

Text itinerary vs mapped announcement

Format What friends see Typical gap
Caption list (“Day 1… Day 4…”) A scroll of names Hard to picture the route
Photo dump Past moments Hides the upcoming path
Animated recap Motion after return Usually not a pre-trip announce
Plotted stop-list map Cities / ports in order Replaces the “which cities again?” moment

Where to share

Group chat, email, Instagram feed or Stories, a work trip channel — you pick. MemoryMap does not schedule posts and does not send the map into those apps for you.

Privacy and judgment

  • Prefer city or port names for public posts. Street-level lodging is rarely needed for an announcement map.
  • Skip exact home addresses and unshared lodging if that feels risky.
  • The public preview is not a permanent public trip gallery. You decide what leaves your device.

FAQ

How do I turn upcoming stops into a postable map?

List each stop with a date and place, plot that list, then screenshot or share a captured frame. MemoryMap does not auto-post.

Do I need every street address?

No. City- or port-level places are enough for an announcement map, and usually wiser for privacy.

Does MemoryMap post this for me?

No. There is no auto-post, scheduler, or “share to social” product button on the public preview.
